PUT YOUR BACKGROUND SONG HERE
=============================

1. Copy your music file into THIS folder (assets/audio/).
2. Keep the file name simple, with no spaces, for example:
      song.mp3
   (other names work too, e.g. our-song.ogg or birthday.mp3)
3. Open data.js in the project root and check the last section
   called "audio". Make sure "src" points to your file, e.g.:
      src: "assets/audio/song.mp3"

Tips:
- MP3 works in every browser. Keep the file small (under 8 MB)
  so it loads quickly on a phone.
- The music NEVER starts by itself - she presses the play button.
- If the file is missing, the play button shows a friendly message
  instead of breaking the website.
